Común Ati

Ending the Stigma

Común Ati supports young people and adults living with mental-health diagnoses through comprehensive care and psychosocial rehabilitation. This campaign was developed to challenge stigma, make the organization’s work more visible, and communicate its mission through respectful, human-centered storytelling. I supported the initiative by developing digital communication strategies, creating social media and promotional content, covering events and collaborations, strengthening fundraising visibility, and translating the organization’s impact into accessible visual stories that could reach new audiences.
